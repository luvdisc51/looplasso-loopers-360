Looplasso Loopers 360

Loopers are floating all around you. Turn, look, and loop your glowing lasso around each one to make friends. In VR your hand is the lasso.

  • Hold the trigger to draw your line, then circle a Looper so the loop closes around it from where you stand.
  • Fill the friendship ring before its timer ends.
  • Protect your line. Bubbles and bumping a Looper snap it. Dodge with your hand.
  • Three waves, seven Loopers, drawn from 51 different Loopers. Fill your Looperdex.
